\# 📱 Mobile Sales Dashboard | Power BI



An interactive \*\*Mobile Sales Dashboard\*\* developed using \*\*Microsoft Power BI\*\* to analyze sales performance, product trends, customer behavior, payment methods, and geographic sales distribution.



The project transforms raw mobile sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard to support data-driven decision-making.



\---



\## 📊 Dashboard Preview



!\[Mobile Sales Dashboard](dashboard-screenshot.png)



\---



\## 🎯 Dashboard Objectives



\* Monitor overall mobile sales performance

\* Analyze sales trends over time

\* Identify top-performing brands and mobile models

\* Analyze city-wise sales performance

\* Understand customer purchasing behavior

\* Compare different payment methods

\* Analyze customer ratings

\* Present business KPIs through interactive visualizations



\---



\## 📈 Key Analysis



\### Sales Performance



\* Total sales

\* Total quantity sold

\* Number of transactions

\* Average sales

\* Monthly and daily sales trends



\### Product Analysis



\* Brand-wise sales

\* Model-wise sales

\* Best-selling mobile models

\* Quantity sold by product



\### Geographic Analysis



\* City-wise sales performance

\* Sales distribution across locations

\* Identification of high-performing cities



\### Customer \& Payment Analysis



\* Customer ratings

\* Payment method distribution

\* Customer purchasing patterns
